# qPCR

## PCR vs RT-PCR vs qPCR vs RT-qPCR

- PCR amplifies DNA targets.
- RT-PCR first converts RNA into cDNA, then amplifies it.
- qPCR measures amplification in real time with fluorescence.
- RT-qPCR combines reverse transcription with real-time quantification and is commonly used for gene expression or viral RNA detection.

## Detection Chemistry

- SYBR Green binds double-stranded DNA and is simple but can detect non-specific amplicons.
- TaqMan probes add sequence specificity and are useful when specificity is critical.
