# Founder Effect

The founder effect occurs when a new population is established by a small number of individuals, causing allele frequencies to differ from the original population.

## Why It Matters

- Rare variants can become relatively common in specific populations.
- It can shape disease prevalence and screening strategies.
- It is related to, but not identical with, genetic drift.

## Examples

- Population-specific pathogenic variants
- Higher prevalence of selected inherited disorders in isolated communities
