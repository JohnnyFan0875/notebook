# HGMD

HGMD, the Human Gene Mutation Database, is a curated database of published germline variants reported in association with human inherited disease.

## Useful For

- Reviewing literature-reported disease-associated germline variants
- Checking whether a variant has prior disease reports
- Supporting variant interpretation when combined with ClinVar, OMIM, gene curation, segregation, phenotype, and population-frequency evidence

## Access

- Website: [https://www.hgmd.cf.ac.uk/](https://www.hgmd.cf.ac.uk/)

## Notes

- HGMD is subscription-based for many use cases.
- A listed variant is not automatically clinically actionable; check the original publication, phenotype match, inheritance model, and current classification evidence.
