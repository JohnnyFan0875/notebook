# GTEx

GTEx, the Genotype-Tissue Expression project, provides human tissue expression and expression quantitative trait locus data.

## Useful For

- Checking whether a gene is expressed in a disease-relevant tissue
- Reviewing tissue-specific expression patterns
- Exploring eQTL evidence linking genetic variants to gene expression

## Access

- Portal: [https://gtexportal.org/](https://gtexportal.org/)

## Notes

- GTEx is strongest for bulk tissue expression and eQTL context.
- Tissue expression supports plausibility, but disease mechanism still needs phenotype, functional, and genetic evidence.
