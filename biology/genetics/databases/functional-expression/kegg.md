# KEGG

KEGG, the Kyoto Encyclopedia of Genes and Genomes, connects genes, proteins, compounds, pathways, diseases, drugs, and organisms.

## Useful For

- Finding pathway context for a gene list
- Connecting genes to metabolic pathways and disease maps
- Supporting enrichment analysis and pathway-level interpretation

## Access

- Website: [https://www.genome.jp/kegg/](https://www.genome.jp/kegg/)

## Notes

- KEGG identifiers and pathway versions should be recorded when used in reproducible analysis.
- Some programmatic or bulk uses may require attention to licensing terms.
