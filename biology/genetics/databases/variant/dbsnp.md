# dbSNP

dbSNP is an NCBI archive of short genetic variants, including single nucleotide variants, small insertions and deletions, and other small-scale sequence changes.

## Useful For

- Mapping a variant to an rsID
- Checking submitted alleles, genomic locations, and links to related NCBI resources
- Normalizing variant identifiers before searching other databases

## Access

- Website: [https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/snp/](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/snp/)
- FTP: [https://ftp.ncbi.nih.gov/snp/](https://ftp.ncbi.nih.gov/snp/)

## Notes

- An rsID is an identifier, not evidence of pathogenicity.
- Confirm genome build, reference allele, alternate allele, and merged/deprecated rsIDs before reporting.
