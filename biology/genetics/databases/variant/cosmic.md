# COSMIC

COSMIC, the Catalogue Of Somatic Mutations In Cancer, collects somatic mutation information observed in human cancers.

## Useful For

- Reviewing recurrent cancer mutations by gene, tumor type, and mutation type
- Separating somatic cancer evidence from inherited germline evidence
- Exploring cancer mutation patterns, genes, samples, and curated literature

## Access

- Website: [https://cancer.sanger.ac.uk/cosmic](https://cancer.sanger.ac.uk/cosmic)

## Notes

- COSMIC is cancer-focused and should not be treated as a general germline pathogenicity database.
- Some downloads and advanced data access may require registration or licensing.
