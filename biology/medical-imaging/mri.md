# MRI

Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) uses magnetic fields and radiofrequency pulses to generate detailed soft-tissue images.

## Common Concepts

- T1- and T2-weighted imaging emphasize different tissue properties.
- Contrast agents may improve lesion characterization.
- MRI is strong for brain, spine, musculoskeletal, and soft-tissue evaluation.

## Notes

- Safety screening for implants and magnetic hazards is essential.
