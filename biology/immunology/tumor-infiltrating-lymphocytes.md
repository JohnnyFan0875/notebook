# Tumor-Infiltrating Lymphocytes (TILs)

**Tumor-infiltrating lymphocytes (TILs)** are immune cells, primarily T cells, that migrate from the bloodstream into the **tumor microenvironment (TME)**. Their presence reflects the host immune response against tumor cells and has important prognostic and therapeutic implications.

## Composition

TILs consist of a heterogeneous population of immune cells, including:

- **CD8⁺ cytotoxic T cells** → directly kill tumor cells by releasing perforin and granzymes.
- **CD4⁺ helper T cells** → coordinate immune responses via cytokine secretion.
- **Regulatory T cells (Tregs)** → suppress anti-tumor immunity and may promote immune evasion.
- **B cells and NK cells** (less common, but can also be present in certain tumors).

## Biological Significance

- **Anti-tumor immunity**: A high density of CD8⁺ TILs is often associated with better clinical outcomes.
- **Immune surveillance**: Presence of TILs suggests the immune system recognizes tumor antigens.
- **Immune evasion**: Tumors can upregulate inhibitory pathways (e.g., PD-L1/PD-1) that suppress TIL activity.

## Clinical Relevance

- **Prognostic value**

  - Many cancers (e.g., melanoma, breast, colorectal) show that increased TIL infiltration correlates with improved survival.
  - TIL density and distribution (intratumoral vs. stromal) are sometimes used as independent prognostic markers.

- **Predictive value**
  - TIL levels can predict responsiveness to **immune checkpoint inhibitors** (e.g., anti-PD-1, anti-CTLA-4).
  - Adoptive TIL therapy (expansion of TILs ex vivo and reinfusion) has shown durable responses in melanoma.

## Assessment Methods

- **Histopathology (H&E staining)** → routine scoring by pathologists.
- **Immunohistochemistry (IHC)** → identifies subsets of TILs (CD8, CD4, FoxP3, etc.).
- **Flow cytometry / single-cell RNA-seq** → detailed phenotyping of TIL populations.

## Challenges & Future Directions

- **Standardization**: Need for harmonized scoring systems across cancer types.
- **Spatial heterogeneity**: TIL density may differ between tumor regions.
- **Functional exhaustion**: Many TILs exhibit an “exhausted” phenotype with reduced cytotoxic activity.
- **Therapeutic targeting**: Strategies aim to reinvigorate TILs via checkpoint inhibitors, cytokine therapy, or cellular engineering (e.g., CAR-T + TIL hybrids).
