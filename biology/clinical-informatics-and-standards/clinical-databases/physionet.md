# PhysioNet

PhysioNet is a platform that hosts biomedical signals, clinical databases, and open research resources.

## What It Provides

- Public and credentialed clinical datasets
- ECG, EEG, waveform, ICU, and challenge datasets
- Documentation, tutorials, and data-use workflows

## Notes

- It is the usual access point for MIMIC and eICU resources.
- Always check the dataset-specific license and training requirements before use.
