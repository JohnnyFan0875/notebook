# eICU

eICU Collaborative Research Database is a multi-center intensive care database that contains de-identified ICU admissions from participating hospitals.

## What It Contains

- ICU patient demographics and admission context
- Vital signs, labs, treatments, and severity scores
- Multi-hospital data that support cross-site comparison

## Notes

- Often accessed through PhysioNet after credentialing.
- Useful for critical care analytics, prediction modeling, and reproducibility exercises.
