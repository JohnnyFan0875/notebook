# MIMIC-IV

MIMIC-IV is a widely used de-identified critical care database derived from Beth Israel Deaconess Medical Center.

## Main Modules

- `hosp`: hospital-level admissions, labs, medications, and billing-related tables
- `icu`: ICU stays, charted events, and device-related data
- `ed`: emergency department encounters

## Notes

- Access is usually managed through PhysioNet training and data-use requirements.
- Common use cases include cohort building, phenotyping, outcomes research, and benchmark datasets.
