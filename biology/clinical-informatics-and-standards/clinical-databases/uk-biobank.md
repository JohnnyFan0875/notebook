# UK Biobank

UK Biobank is a large prospective cohort that links genotype, phenotype, imaging, and health record data.

## Highlights

- Large-scale population cohort with long-term follow-up
- Rich clinical, lifestyle, and imaging metadata
- Commonly used for epidemiology, genetics, and polygenic risk research

## Notes

- Access requires an approved application and data-use agreement.
- Cohort composition and ascertainment should be considered during interpretation.
