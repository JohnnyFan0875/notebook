# Ontology Lookup Service

Ontology Lookup Service (OLS) provides searchable access to biomedical ontologies and controlled vocabularies.

## Useful For

- Looking up ontology terms and identifiers
- Exploring hierarchical relationships between concepts
- Supporting standardized annotation in research datasets

## Access

- Website: [https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4/)
