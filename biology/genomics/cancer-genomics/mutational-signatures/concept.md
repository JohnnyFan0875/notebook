# Mutational Signature Concept

Mutational signatures summarize recurring mutation patterns left by biological processes such as aging, APOBEC activity, UV exposure, tobacco smoke, or defective DNA repair.

## Main Categories

- SBS: single base substitution signatures
- DBS: doublet base substitution signatures
- ID: small insertion and deletion signatures

## Why They Matter

- Help infer underlying mutational mechanisms
- Support tumor biology interpretation
- Provide context for clinically relevant repair pathways
