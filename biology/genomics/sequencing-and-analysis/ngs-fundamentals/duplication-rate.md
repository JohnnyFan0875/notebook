# Duplication Rate

Duplication rate describes the proportion of reads that appear redundant, often because of PCR amplification or limited library complexity.

## Why It Matters

- High duplication reduces unique usable coverage.
- It can indicate over-amplification or insufficient input complexity.
- Interpretation depends on assay type, especially for targeted panels.

## Common Review Step

- Check duplication metrics from Picard or related tools after alignment.
