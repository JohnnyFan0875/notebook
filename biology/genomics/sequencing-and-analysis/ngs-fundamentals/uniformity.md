# Uniformity

Uniformity describes how evenly sequencing reads cover the intended target regions.

## Why It Matters

- Good uniformity reduces dropout regions.
- It improves confidence that the panel or exome is performing consistently.
- Poor uniformity can lower sensitivity even when the mean depth looks high.

## Common Checks

- Percent of targets above threshold depth
- Fold-80 base penalty or similar assay-specific metrics
