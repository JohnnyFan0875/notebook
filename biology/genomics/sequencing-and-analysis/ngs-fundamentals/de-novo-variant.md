# De Novo Variant vs Novel Variant

These terms are related but not interchangeable.

## De Novo Variant

- A variant that is present in the proband but absent in the biological parents.
- Usually assessed with trio sequencing and careful inheritance review.
- Often important in rare disease and neurodevelopmental disorder studies.

## Novel Variant

- A variant that is not found in the reference set or databases being used for comparison.
- Novel does not automatically mean de novo or pathogenic.

## Practical Note

- A variant can be novel without being de novo, and de novo without being globally novel.
