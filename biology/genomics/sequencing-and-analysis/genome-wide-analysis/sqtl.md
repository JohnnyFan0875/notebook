# sQTL

Splicing quantitative trait loci (sQTL) are variants associated with alternative splicing patterns.

## Key Ideas

- They affect exon usage, splice junctions, or transcript isoforms.
- They may explain disease mechanisms not visible in total gene expression.
- They are often analyzed alongside eQTL data.

## Notes

- Tools such as LeafCutter are commonly used in sQTL workflows.
