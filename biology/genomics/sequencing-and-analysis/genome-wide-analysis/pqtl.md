# pQTL

Protein quantitative trait loci (pQTL) are variants associated with protein abundance.

## Why They Matter

- They connect genetics to downstream protein-level biology.
- They can complement eQTL analyses when transcript and protein effects differ.
- They are useful in target prioritization and causal inference studies.

## Notes

- Interpretation depends on assay platform, tissue context, and cohort design.
- 統計框架與 eQTL 相同（族群結構 PCs、多重檢定），見 [QTL Analysis and Mapping](qtl-analysis-and-mapping.md#相關統計方法)；aptamer 平台的 cis-pQTL 可能是 variant 改變抗原決定位造成的結合假象。
