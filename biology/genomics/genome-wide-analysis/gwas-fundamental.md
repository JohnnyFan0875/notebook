# GWAS Fundamentals

Genome-wide association studies (GWAS) test whether genetic variants are associated with a phenotype across the genome.

## Core Ideas

- Millions of variants may be tested in a single study.
- Population structure, sample size, and phenotype definition strongly affect results.
- Multiple-testing correction is essential because of the large search space.

## Common Outputs

- Manhattan plot
- QQ plot
- Effect sizes, confidence intervals, and p-values
