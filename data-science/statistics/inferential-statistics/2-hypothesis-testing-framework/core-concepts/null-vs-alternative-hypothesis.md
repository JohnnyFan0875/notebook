## Null and Alternative Hypotheses

All hypothesis tests begin with two competing statements:

| Hypothesis                 | Symbol     | Interpretation                                           |
| -------------------------- | ---------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Null hypothesis**        | H₀         | No effect, no difference, or status quo (e.g., μ₁ = μ₂). |
| **Alternative hypothesis** | H₁ (or Hₐ) | An effect or difference exists (e.g., μ₁ ≠ μ₂).          |

**Decision rule:**  
Use a test statistic (z, t, F, χ²) to compare observed data against expectations under H₀.  
If the probability (p-value) of observing such data under H₀ is ≤ α, reject H₀ in favor of H₁.
