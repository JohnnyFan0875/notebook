# Sampling and Distributions

This section introduces the foundation of **statistical inference** — how we draw conclusions about a population from a sample.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand the role of **sampling** in inference.
- Explain how the **Central Limit Theorem (CLT)** justifies normal approximations.
- Distinguish between **standard deviation** and **standard error**.
- Construct and interpret **confidence intervals** for population parameters.
