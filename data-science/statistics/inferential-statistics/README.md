# Inferential Statistics

**Inferential statistics** provide the foundation for making conclusions about populations based on sample data.  
This section explains the logic, assumptions, and applications of statistical inference — from sampling theory to hypothesis testing, test selection, and effect size interpretation.

## Learning Objectives

- Understand how sampling distributions form the basis of statistical inference.
- Apply the **Central Limit Theorem (CLT)** to justify normal approximations.
- Construct and interpret **confidence intervals**.
- Design and perform **hypothesis tests** (parametric and non-parametric).
- Choose appropriate statistical tests based on data type and assumptions.
- Quantify and report **effect sizes** and **statistical power**.

- Always verify assumptions before running statistical tests:
  - [Normality](./2-hypothesis-testing-framework/assumption-tests/normality-tests.md)
