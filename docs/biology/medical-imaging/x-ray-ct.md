# X-ray and CT

X-ray provides projection images, while computed tomography (CT) reconstructs cross-sectional anatomy from X-ray measurements.

## Comparison

- X-ray is fast and simple for initial structural assessment.
- CT offers higher anatomic detail and 3D context.
- CT uses more radiation and may involve intravenous contrast.

## Notes

- Hounsfield units are central to CT interpretation and tissue characterization.
