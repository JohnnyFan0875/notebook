# MSDS and SDS

Material Safety Data Sheet (MSDS) and Safety Data Sheet (SDS) documents describe hazards, handling, storage, and emergency response information for chemicals and laboratory materials.

## Typical Sections

- Hazard identification
- Composition and ingredient information
- First-aid and firefighting measures
- Handling, storage, and exposure control

## Notes

- Modern systems usually use the SDS format aligned with GHS.
- Always pair the document with local workplace safety procedures.
