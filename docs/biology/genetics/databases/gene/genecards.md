# GeneCards

GeneCards is a gene-centric knowledge portal that aggregates summaries, expression data, disease links, pathways, and external database references.

## Useful For

- Quick orientation on a gene of interest
- Jumping to linked resources such as UniProt, OMIM, and HGNC
- Reviewing expression, function, and disease association summaries

## Notes

- It is convenient for overview browsing but should be cross-checked with primary databases for formal interpretation.
