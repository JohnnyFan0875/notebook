# ClinVar

ClinVar is a public archive of reports on the clinical significance of genetic variants. It aggregates submissions from clinical laboratories, researchers, and expert panels.

## Key Features

- Clinical significance assertions such as pathogenic, likely pathogenic, VUS, likely benign, and benign
- Supporting evidence from submitters and review groups
- Links to genes, conditions, and external identifiers
