# Human Protein Atlas

The Human Protein Atlas (HPA) is a resource for exploring tissue, cell-type, and subcellular protein expression patterns.

## Useful For

- Reviewing tissue-specific expression
- Checking antibody-based protein localization data
- Comparing RNA and protein-level evidence

## Access

- [Human Protein Atlas tissue resource](https://www.proteinatlas.org/humanproteome/tissue)
